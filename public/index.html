Tether

Quietly always-on. Nobody's cloud but yours.

A chat app for DMs and communities that runs on infrastructure you own. There is no Tether company holding your messages — each community runs its own backend, and the app is just the client that connects to it.

Download for macOS Download for Windows

Desktop, v1 — macOS and Windows. Builds are published on GitHub Releases; until the first one lands you can run it from source.

Messages wait for you

Turn your machine off. Messages sent meanwhile are held until you come back, then land with a native notification the moment you're online. No peer-to-peer coin flip about whether both people happened to be awake at once.

It cleans up after itself

A direct message is deleted once it has actually been delivered, and expires on its own if it never is. What's stored is what's in flight — not a permanent transcript of everything anyone ever said.

Your server, your project

Starting a community means standing up your own backend. Your growth is your own; nobody else's community shares your quota, and nobody can switch yours off.

Works on any network

No port forwarding, no NAT wrangling, no static IP. The app only ever makes outbound connections, so it works from behind carrier-grade NAT and locked-down networks alike.

Tether is open source — github.com/gremstard/tether
